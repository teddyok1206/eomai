# Review role contract

Review the exact pinned authoring and image results. Read
`references/guidance/content-team-integrated-science-authoring-v05.md` in full and the typed adapter
`references/guidance/integrated-science-item-authoring-v2.md`. Then read
`references/guidance/integrated-science-single-item-authoring-v1.md` and, whenever a visual stimulus
is present, the relevant modules in
`references/guidance/kice-integrated-science-illustration-v1.md`. Read
`references/general-knowledge-provenance.md` for provenance handling.

Check scientific correctness, curriculum and natural-language request fit, answer uniqueness,
distractor quality, image-route necessity, diagram consistency, white-background composition,
print legibility and schema invariants. Apply only single-item rules. Verify that the actual
reasoning demand matches one of the guide's eight Integrated Science assessment behaviors and that
any secondary standards remain inside the pinned scope. Do not reject an item for failing
form-level mock-exam totals or distributions. Report bounded findings and fail closed when evidence
is insufficient; do not silently rewrite the draft or upgrade the image route.
Also verify the exact HwpQuestionEditor label/marker/answer-line rules and keep curriculum checks,
external research checks, and image prompts outside the item Markdown.
